What We Fix

Full-service plumbing, priced 4 Less

From a dripping faucet at 2pm to a flooded basement at 2am, one licensed crew handles the whole job — diagnosis, repair and cleanup — for a price you approve before a wrench turns.

Emergency Leak & Burst Pipe Repair

Shut-off, isolation and permanent repair for split copper, cracked PEX and frozen lines. We stop the water first, then fix the cause and pressure-test the run before we leave.

  • Slab & wall leak detection
  • Emergency shut-off service
  • Pressure testing included

Drain Cleaning & Clog Removal

Kitchen, bath, laundry and floor drains cleared by cable machine or hydro-jet — plus a camera check so a recurring clog doesn't send you searching for a plumber again next month.

  • Hydro-jetting for grease lines
  • In-line camera inspection
  • Root intrusion removal

Water Heater Repair & Installation

No hot water tonight? We service and replace gas, electric and tankless units, haul the old tank away, and bring the connections, pan and venting up to code.

  • Same-day tank replacement
  • Tankless service & descaling
  • Thermostat & element repair

Fixture & Faucet Installation

Faucets, toilets, garbage disposals, shower valves, laundry boxes and outdoor spigots — installed cleanly, sealed properly and left leak-free and tested.

  • Toilet rebuild & replacement
  • Disposal & shower valve swaps
  • Customer-supplied fixtures welcome

Sewer & Main Line Service

Sewage backing up into the tub is an emergency, and we treat it like one. Camera locate, clear the blockage, then a straight answer on whether it needs a spot repair or a full line.

  • Backup clearing & sanitizing
  • Locate & depth marking
  • Spot repair or line replacement

Repiping & Water Line Repair

Rusty water, weak pressure or a leak you keep patching? We repipe homes in PEX or copper and replace failing service lines from the meter to the house.

  • Whole-home PEX & copper repiping
  • Main water line replacement
  • Pressure regulator & valve work
